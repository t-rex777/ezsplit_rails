    Questions, answered.

    How do I join ezsplit?

    ezsplit is invite-only. Ask a friend who uses it to send you an invite link, or scan their QR invite, then sign up in the app.

    Can I split a bill unevenly?

    Yes. Split equally, by percentage, or by exact amounts. You see each person's share before you save.

    Does ezsplit move money?

    No. ezsplit keeps track of who owes whom. You pay each other however you usually do, then record the payment in the app.

    Can someone change an expense without me knowing?

    Every add, edit, delete and settle-up shows in Activity, with who did it, when, and what changed.
